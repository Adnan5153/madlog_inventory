The Old Way vs The Madlog Way

Workshops lose hours every week to manual stock checks, misplaced parts, and rushed reorders. Madlog replaces that chaos with a single source of truth.

The Old Way

  • Spreadsheets that nobody updates until something is missing.
  • Walk-ins to the storeroom just to confirm a part exists.
  • Last-minute emergency orders that eat margins.
  • Job cards with no link to the parts consumed.
  • Stock variance discovered at month-end, not in time to act.
  • Zero visibility for owners managing more than one bay.

The Madlog Way

  • Live stock counts that update with every receipt and issue.
  • Bin-level locations so storekeepers walk straight to the part.
  • Automatic reorder thresholds with suggested purchase orders.
  • Every job card tied to the exact parts and quantities used.
  • Daily variance report so problems surface within 24 hours.
  • Multi-bay dashboard with one consolidated view for owners.

Everything a Workshop Store Needs

Issue to Job Card

Low-Stock Thresholds

HOW IT FITS YOUR DAY

From Receiving to Reorder

Five steps that match the rhythm of a working storeroom — without paper ledgers or clipboards.

  1. 01

    Receive

    Record deliveries against a purchase order. Quantities and costs update instantly.

  2. 02

    Store

    Bin locations and quantities are locked in. Search by part number, name, or bin code.

  3. 03

    Issue

    Issue parts to a job card in two clicks. The job card shows live cost and margin.

  4. 04

    Use

    Mechanics see what was used and what remains. Nothing is silently pulled from stock.

  5. 05

    Reorder

    Low-stock alerts trigger suggested POs. Approve, send, done — before the part runs out.
